# Humblepaper

Humblepaper, Inc. builds [Leela](https://leela.works), a contract negotiation agent that works over email, and maintains [The Contract Room](https://thecontractroom.com), an open reference to commercial deal-making. The repositories below are the reference source and the lists maintained alongside it.

- [contract-room-ontology](https://github.com/HumblePaper/contract-room-ontology): the reference source, an open and cited ontology (CC BY 4.0). Pull requests welcome. Merges go live on the site.
- [awesome-contract-negotiation](https://github.com/HumblePaper/awesome-contract-negotiation): the working materials of commercial contract negotiation, from model forms and playbooks to tools and communities.
- [awesome-ai-native-law-firms](https://github.com/HumblePaper/awesome-ai-native-law-firms): law firms that practise with AI as the method of work, arranged by how each firm is constituted.
- [awesome-people-in-legal-tech](https://github.com/HumblePaper/awesome-people-in-legal-tech): the people who build, fund, report on and study legal technology, cross-referenced with the other lists.
- [awesome-legal-ai-benchmarks](https://github.com/HumblePaper/awesome-legal-ai-benchmarks): the benchmarks, leaderboards, rubrics, harnesses and datasets by which AI is measured on legal work.
